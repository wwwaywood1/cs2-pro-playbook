# REVIEW-02 — heatmaps and tactic identification

## Contract changes and numerical corrections

- **Additive field:** `summary[steamid][side].utility.spots[].level` is now emitted (`0` upper, `1` lower), matching the existing landing-spot and heat-grid convention. Previously these summary markers had coordinates but no floor. `web/src/types.ts::UtilitySpot` and `DESIGN.md` are updated. No existing JSON field is renamed or removed.
- **Player tactic proportions:** `T.lurk_share` and `CT.forward_share` now divide by that player's eligible played rounds, across his teams, rather than the rounds of his modal team. This corrects transfer/substitution denominators; previously a transfer could produce a proportion above 1. Profiles remain one row per Steam ID, and their `team` remains the modal team.
- Holding measures three-dimensional displacement, as implied by “moving less than 40 units”; vertical travel is no longer stationary time. Invalid plant/event times and floor mixing are corrected without changing the documented field meanings.
- Density-cluster tie breaking is now coordinate-based. Re-export can change spot IDs, memberships and downstream recipes in tied/overlapping cases. Regenerate the complete site together; do not mix old spot/lineup references with new JSON.

## Other findings, ranked by impact

1. **P1 — Export-aborting non-finite coordinates. Fixed.** `_cells` cast NaN/infinity to integers before filtering, raising instead of dropping an unusable event. All three coordinates must now be finite before grid conversion; landing clustering also filters invalid XYZ. Four parameterized regressions cover NaN, both infinities and null in a non-X coordinate. This is localized cleaning, not a claim that every malformed input table is supported.
2. **P1 — Cross-team player tactic denominator. Fixed.** Explained above; an event numerator covering two teams could be divided by only one team's rounds. Own played-round eligibility also avoids diluting substitutes by rounds they did not play. A direct regression covers both T and CT proportions.
3. **P2 — Freeze-time kills/deaths and grenade throws counted as live heatmap events. Fixed.** `is_post_round` alone does not exclude negative round-relative time. Kills/deaths now require `t_s >= 0`; utility requires `throw_t_s >= 0`, consistent with shots and positions. Regression: `test_preround_kills_and_utility_are_not_counted`.
4. **P2 — Unknown-callout holding summary can crash on tied clusters. Fixed.** Sorting equal-size holds compared `None` to a string. The sort now handles unknown callouts and includes floor/coordinates. Regression: `test_holding_clusters_with_unknown_callout_do_not_crash_on_tied_counts`.
5. **P2 — Early lurk can use future positions. Fixed.** The precise before/after case is in row 8. This changes tactic labels, not just presentation.
6. **Residual limitations.** The lineup library still clusters release/landing positions in XY, as documented; that is a separate lineup-definition change and is not silently redefined here. One-second snapshots cannot resolve motion or death/respawn entirely between samples. Density neighbour storage remains potentially quadratic for very dense clouds; chunking bounds the distance workspace, not the total neighbour list. No claim of performance validation over hundreds of real demos is made.
